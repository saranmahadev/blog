Imaxt

Typography and layout as the visuals. Posts here have no images: figures, numbers and diagrams are made of type.

How to use it

Write the post as .mdx. Every block below is available without imports, and a post that uses none of them stays plain Markdown.

Rule of thumbAt most one loud block per section. Imaxt should punctuate the writing, not replace it.

Statement

A poster-sized sentence. Wrap words in **bold** for the marker highlight and *italic* for the expressive italic.
