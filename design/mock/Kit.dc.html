The kit

Ink is the primary colour and emerald is the accent. Three layers, one rule each. Structure is brutalist, content tiles are slabs, controls are clay. Use the Tweaks panel to compare accent colours (emerald is the default) and to preview dark mode.

03 · Surfaces: the three layers

Structure
Rules and grid

2px ink rules, square corners, no shadow. Dividers, columns, list rows and section headers.

Slab
Flat, bordered, hard shadow

2px border, 14px radius, 5px offset shadow, no blur. Lead tiles, series, callouts. Presses 2px on hover.

Clay
Soft and tactile

Dual soft shadows plus inner highlights. Buttons, pills, switches, avatars. Pressed state goes inset.

09 · Layout, spacing and motion

Width
  • Content cap 1680px, gutters clamp(16px, 3vw, 48px)
  • Bands run edge to edge
  • Article: 260 / text / 300 columns, text about 68 characters
  • Wide blocks may extend 60px into the gutters
Breakpoints
  • 1280 and up: three-column article, 12-column home
  • 1024: rails collapse under the text
  • 768: home stacks, feed to two columns
  • Below 640: single column, action dock at bottom
Motion and a11y
  • Slabs press 2px on hover, 120ms
  • Clay buttons go inset on press
  • All motion off for reduced-motion users
  • Focus ring: 3px signal blue, 2px offset. Targets 44px or more
