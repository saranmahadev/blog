Diagrams

Write a Mermaid diagram in a mermaid fenced block, in any .md or .mdx post. It is drawn to SVG at build time (flowchart, sequence, state, class, ER, Gantt, timeline, mindmap, pie and more), skinned to the Imaxt look, and switches with the light and dark theme. Every diagram must have accTitle and accDescr; the build fails without them. Add caption="..." or wide after the language. Live examples: flowchart, sequence, state.
