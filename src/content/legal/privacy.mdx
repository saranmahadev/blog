**Drafted is built to collect as little about you as possible, and this page lists all of it.**

## In short

- No accounts, no sign-up and no comments.
- No cookies, no advertising and no analytics.
- No third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. Everything loads from this site.
- Search and diagrams run in your own browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
- Three things are processed, all listed in the table below: technical request data, any email you send me, and one setting saved on your own device.

## Who is responsible

**Saran Mahadev**, an individual based in India, runs this site and decides how personal data is handled (a "data fiduciary" under Indian law, a "controller" under the GDPR). You can reach me at [mail@saranmahadev.in](mailto:mail@saranmahadev.in).

## What is processed, why, where, and for how long

| What | Why | Where it lives | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Your IP address and the technical details your browser sends with every request**, such as the page you asked for, your browser type and the page you came from | To deliver the site, detect abuse and keep it running. Google uses IP addresses of incoming requests to detect abuse and to give site owners aggregate usage figures | Google Firebase Hosting, which acts as my processor | Google states that Hosting retains IP data for a few months. I do not keep my own copy or build profiles of visitors |
| **Your email address and your message**, only if you write to me | To read your message, reply, and keep a record of the conversation | My email account | For as long as the conversation is useful. I will delete it earlier if you ask |
| **One item called `theme`** in your browser's local storage, only if you switch between light and dark mode | To remember your choice on your next visit | Your own device. It is never sent to me | Until you clear your browser data |

I can see aggregate figures such as request counts and bandwidth in the Firebase console. I do not use them to identify anyone.

## What this site does not do

- It sets no cookies.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or fingerprinting.
- It does not load anything from other companies' servers, such as fonts, scripts, maps or social buttons.
- It does not sell, rent or share personal data, and does not use it to profile you or make automated decisions about you.

If this ever changes, for example by adding privacy-friendly analytics, this page will be updated **first**, and will say exactly what is collected, by whom and why.

## Who else handles your data

Only **Google**, through Firebase Hosting, which serves the pages. Google acts as my processor for that service. It runs on Google's global infrastructure, so a request may be handled in any country where Google operates data centers, which may not be your own. I have not selected a specific region. I will share personal data with anyone else only if the law requires it.

## Links to other sites

Posts link to outside sources, and the footer links to my other profiles, such as YouTube and Instagram. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which I do not control. Nothing is loaded from them until you choose to follow a link.

## Your rights

**In India.** The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 are being brought into force in stages, with the rights below expected to apply from **May 2027**. I follow them already. You may ask me:

1. For a summary of the personal data I hold about you and how it is used.
2. To correct, complete, update or erase it.
3. To address a grievance about how it is handled.
4. To nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.

**In the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom.** You also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability under the GDPR and UK GDPR, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

**How to use them.** Email [mail@saranmahadev.in](mailto:mail@saranmahadev.in) and say what you would like. I may ask you to confirm who you are first, and I aim to reply within **30 days**, which is the period the GDPR sets. If you are not satisfied with my answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, once it is receiving complaints, or to your local authority.

## Legal basis

- **For visitors covered by the GDPR:** hosting data is processed under my legitimate interest in running and securing the site, and email under my legitimate interest in answering you, or your consent when you choose to write.
- **In India:** the data is processed for the purposes you provided it for, or for the legitimate uses the DPDP Act allows.
