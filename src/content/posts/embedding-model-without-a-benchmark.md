You rarely have a proper evaluation set on day one. You still have to pick a model.

## A practical checklist

1. write twenty real questions your users would ask
2. mark which documents should answer each one
3. try two or three models and count how often the right document is in the top five

It is crude, but it is better than choosing by leaderboard.
