You rarely have a proper evaluation set on day one. You still have to pick a model.

<Statement tone="sky">Twenty real questions are worth more than **any leaderboard**.</Statement>

## A practical checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the questions">Twenty real ones your users would ask, in their words.</Step>
  <Step title="Mark the answers">For each question, note which documents should come back.</Step>
  <Step title="Count the hits">Try two or three models and count how often the right document is in the top five.</Step>
</Steps>
