<Sidenote label="Note">This is a welcome note, not a story. It says what Drafted is and how it works.</Sidenote>

<Statement>Drafted is where I think **in writing**.</Statement>

It is the writing layer of my work. An idea starts rough in my head, and it only becomes something I can check, and something you can check, once it is written down in full sentences. The name is honest: most of what you read here began as a draft and is still being improved.

## What I Write About

Software engineering, AI and technology, productivity, systems, entrepreneurship, careers, lessons from projects, experiments, and long-form technical writing.

Some posts will be a worked-out explanation. Some will be a story about how I got something wrong. Every post says which kind it is at the top, so you never have to guess whether a scene really happened.

## Where to Start

There are three series, and you can start with any of them.

- **[Building Drafted](/building-drafted/)** is how this site was made, one decision at a time: what I chose, why, and what broke.
- **[Building Dev Universe](/building-dev-universe/)** is the larger system around my work, beginning with a map of all the parts.
- **Curious to Coder** is coming soon. It is about the curiosity that quietly points people toward code.

## How to Read It

You do not need an account to read anything here. If you want more, there are a few ways in, and each one is optional.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Just read">No account, no ads, no tracking scripts. Pages are plain files, so they load fast.</Step>
  <Step title="Follow">Every post, every series and every topic has its own feed. Paste [/rss.xml](/rss.xml) into any feed reader.</Step>
  <Step title="Come back">Bookmarks and the place you stopped reading are saved in your browser first. Make an account and they follow you to other devices.</Step>
  <Step title="Write to me">Under every post there is a private message box. Only you and I can see what you send, and I reply to you there.</Step>
</Steps>

## Why There Are No Pictures

Posts here use no images. Where another site would put a screenshot or an illustration, I use type and layout: a big sentence, a before and after, a small diagram. It is a strict rule on purpose, and the first post in *Building Drafted* explains why.

Thank you for reading. Pick a series, or just wander.
