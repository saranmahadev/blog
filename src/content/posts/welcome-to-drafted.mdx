<Statement>Drafted is where ideas are developed **in writing**.</Statement>

Some ideas only become clear when you try to say them in full sentences. Drafted is where I do that: the writing and thinking layer of my work, built for deep thinking and written knowledge.

## Who Writes It

I am Dev: creator, engineer, educator, builder and entrepreneur. Drafted is one part of a larger system of work that I describe in [Dev Universe](/dev-universe/), and you can read a little more on the [About](/about/) page.

## What You Will Find

Software engineering, AI and technology, productivity, systems, entrepreneurship, careers, lessons from projects, experiments, and long-form technical writing. Some posts are explanations. Some are stories from my own experience. Both are written to be useful when you have the time to read them properly.

## How a Post Is Written

<Compare>
  <Side kind="before" label="What Drafted is not">A stream of quick takes, or a way of repeating what has already been said.</Side>
  <Side label="What Drafted is">One idea per post, explained plainly, with a diagram or a block where a picture of type helps. Every claim is something I can stand behind.</Side>
</Compare>

Where a post teaches something, it defines each term the first time it appears and says when the idea does not apply. Where a post is a story, it comes from something that actually happened.

## Reading Along

You can read everything here without an account. To follow new posts, use the feed at [/rss.xml](/rss.xml) in any feed reader, or simply come back. If you want to talk about a post, there is a private message box under each one: only you and I can see what you write, and I reply there.

Thank you for reading.
