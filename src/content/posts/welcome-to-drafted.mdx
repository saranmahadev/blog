**Hello, and thank you for being here.**

I'm Saran Mahadev: creator, engineer, educator, builder and entrepreneur. Drafted is where I think out loud, slowly, on the page.

Every idea I have ever been proud of started as a bad draft. A tangled paragraph, half a sentence, a note I almost did not keep. Writing is how those turn into something I can stand behind. The name is a small promise to myself: nothing here has to be finished to be worth sharing. It only has to be honest.

## What Drafted is

A home for ideas written out in full sentences. Software engineering, AI and technology, productivity, systems, entrepreneurship, careers, lessons from projects and the occasional experiment.

Some posts explain how something works. Some are stories from things that actually happened to me. Both are written to be useful, and, where I can manage it, a little warm.

## What you can expect

- **One idea per post,** explained plainly, with a diagram or a block of code where a picture helps.
- **Terms defined the first time they appear,** and a note on when an idea does not apply.
- **Honest limits.** When I am unsure, I say so, and I mark which parts are my opinion.
- **No account, no paywall, no noise.** Just come and read.

> **A small promise.** Where a post teaches something, I will try to make it something you can use tomorrow. Where it is a story, it will be a true one.

## Start anywhere

There is no right order. If you want a few doors to try:

- [From LLMs That Talk to Models That Decide](/posts/from-llms-to-decision-models/) is a short tour of a new idea in AI, and a project of mine that used it.
- [Deterministic Engineering](/series/deterministic-engineering/) is a six-part series on why the AI era needs more verification, not less.
