Content is the entry point into Dev Universe. The idea is that different platforms serve different purposes, instead of simply publishing the same material everywhere.

## Three Platforms, Three Jobs

### Dev Drafted: deep thinking

[Dev Drafted](/) is the writing and thinking layer, and you are reading it. It is where ideas are developed in written form: software engineering, AI and technology, productivity, systems, entrepreneurship, careers, project lessons, experiments and long-form technical writing.

**Its job: deep thinking and written knowledge.**

### YouTube: learn, build and share

[Saran Mahadev on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@saranmahadev) is meant to be the primary public-facing content channel. It focuses on technology, AI, software engineering, productivity, business, projects, experiments, tutorials, long-form videos and Shorts.

**Its job: learn, build and share.**

### Everything.by.Dev: discovery

[Everything.by.Dev](https://www.instagram.com/everything.by.dev) on Instagram is the discovery and distribution layer. It is not another personal account. It is a feed of what is happening across Dev Universe: new videos, articles, courses, workshops, podcast episodes, products, community updates, discoveries and educational content.
