Vazhviyal expands Dev Universe beyond technology.

## The Idea

The concept is to explore life through conversations with interesting people. The subjects can include technology, entrepreneurship, science, careers, finance, creativity, psychology, education, relationships, leadership, failure, philosophy, culture and personal journeys.

## The Objective

The objective is not merely to interview successful people. It is to:

<PullQuote>Spend meaningful time with interesting people and understand what they know about life.</PullQuote>

## How It Connects

Everything else in Dev Universe is about what I know and what I build. This is the part where the knowledge comes from someone else. After conversations comes the question of what happens to the people who listen: [Community](/building-dev-universe/community/).
