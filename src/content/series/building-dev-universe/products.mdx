The product layer is planned at shop.saranmahadev.in. It turns knowledge and intellectual property into useful things people can actually use.

## What Could Be Sold

Potential products include templates, developer tools, Notion and Obsidian systems, productivity systems, engineering resources, AI resources, e-books, guides, checklists, code, courses, toolkits and, eventually, SaaS products.

## The Principle

<PullQuote>Content teaches. Products help people execute.</PullQuote>

A post can explain how to do something. A template, a toolkit or a system lets you do it on Monday morning without starting from a blank page.

## How It Connects

Products are where the other layers become useful in someone's own work. The most ambitious idea in this set sits on the knowledge side: [the AI Library](/building-dev-universe/the-ai-library/).
