A series is a promise: here are several posts that belong together. The least I can do is help you keep your place in one.

## The Series Page

Every series has its own page, with its title, a short description and the parts listed in reading order. On it there is a **Your progress** box that counts the parts you have read, like **2 / 4**, with a bar underneath.

Below the count is one button that takes you where you should go next. If you have not started, it says **Start reading**. Otherwise it says **Continue reading**, with the title of the part it will open.

## How "Next" Is Chosen

The button is not just the next number. It follows a simple rule:

<Steps>
  <Step title="A part you are part way through">If you have started one but not finished it, that is where you go, and the furthest-along one wins.</Step>
  <Step title="Otherwise, the first part you have not read">Skipped something earlier? The button takes you back to it.</Step>
  <Step title="If you have read everything">The box says so, and the button goes away.</Step>
</Steps>

A part counts as **read** once you have been through about nine tenths of it.

## On the Post Itself

When you are reading one part of a series, the left side lists every part with the current one in bold, and a percentage or **Read** beside the ones you have started. At the bottom of the page there are **Previous** and **Up next** cards, so you never have to go back to the series page to move on.

Each series also has its own feed, so you can follow just that series in a feed reader.

## And the Same Rules Apply

Series progress comes from the same place as bookmarks: your browser first, your account if you are signed in. Nothing about it needs an account, and nothing about it is visible to anyone else.

**A series should be easy to leave and easy to come back to.**
